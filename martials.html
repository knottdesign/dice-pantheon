Set One — Mixed Metal

The Martials

Warriors rely on their weapons and the strength of their arms. For the Martials we chose heavy solid metals — cold to the touch, heavy in the hand, and landing on the table with a thunk you feel through the wood.

The Frontline

King of metals — light, non-corrosive, notoriously difficult to machine. We chose it for the D20 because it represents the leader: unbreakable and tireless.
