The Premise

Your character is legendary.
Your dice should be too.

A hero is an adventurer who gains power through perseverance — their spirit defined by their party, their deeds, and their class. So we built a die for each one, and let the metal carry the meaning.

Most metal dice are cast from a single uniform alloy. Dice Pantheon is machined — industrial multi-axis CNC work that carves a unique density and weight for every single die, then finishes each one differently. Seven processes in all, from multi-colour electroplating to industrial chemical bluing to forced antique patinas.

Set One

The Martials

Warriors rely on their weapons and the strength of their arms. For the Martials we chose heavy solid metals — cold to the touch, heavy in the hand, and landing on the table with a satisfying thunk.

Titanium · Stainless Steel · Copper · Brass

Every die weighed — mixed metal set

D20

The Frontline

TC4 Titanium · Fine Polished

Titanium is the king of metals — light, non-corrosive, notoriously difficult to machine. We chose it for the D20 because it represents the leader: unbreakable and tireless.

D12

The Barbarian

Stainless Steel · Stonewashed

A battle-worn finish drawing on primal rage. Like a greataxe that has seen a thousand wars — gritty, textured, and ready to cleave.

D100

The Fighter

Stainless Steel · Mirror Polish

No tricks, just mastery. The Fighter is the backbone of the party and steel is the backbone of the world — polished to perfection. Heavy, cold, precise.

D10

The Ranger

Pure Copper · Forced Patina

Rangers don't need to shine; they need to survive. No two are alike — and as a living metal, this copper keeps ageing into a patina entirely its own.

D8

The Paladin

Solid Brass · Mirror Finish

Shining like gold in the face of adversity, with the holy aura of a divine knight. Heavy in the hand, it carries the weight of your oath.

D6

The Rogue

Chemically Blued Steel

Draped in shadow. An industrial bluing process — historically used to darken blades — eliminates glint. Your silent partner when striking from the dark.

D4

The Monk

Solid Brass · Brushed

Brass for the bruisers. Unlike the Paladin's shine, this finish feels like an ancient temple gong or a hardened knuckle duster. It strikes with distinct impact.

The Centrepiece

The Titan

We didn't just want to make a big die; we wanted to machine a monolith. Milled from a single massive billet of aerospace TC4 titanium, it towers over standard dice and dominates the table.

With a crushing weight of 121.07 grams, the Titan carries an inertia no other material can match. It doesn't roll — it collides. When it lands, the fate of the party is sealed.

Our Story

It started with a campaign
and an MBA.

We met in Toronto during our MBA — a bond cemented not just by shared projects, but by a shared love of Dungeons & Dragons. It became the game that connected us and the creative outlet that kept us inspired.

Dice Pantheon is the natural intersection of our professional expertise and our personal passion: the dice sets of our dreams, made to elevate the experience every time we play.

Mandana

Over ten years in R&D and architecture. Brings an obsession with structure and material science, ensuring every die is a precision-engineered instrument.

Catherine

A decade of expertise in operations. Makes certain every detail — from the weight in your hand to the final experience — is seamless.

Parmeet

Has run D&D games for nearly a decade, carrying the hobby across many adventures. Ensures every design upholds the spirit of the game and feels as good to roll as it looks to display.
