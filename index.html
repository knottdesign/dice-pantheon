What Dice Pantheon Is

A die for every class.

Fifteen dice for Dungeons & Dragons, where the material matches the mettle of the class that rolls it. Titanium for the leader, blued steel for the rogue, copper that keeps ageing for the ranger.

Most metal dice are poured from one alloy and tumbled. Every die here is machined from solid stock on multi‑axis CNC and finished by a process chosen for its class — which is the whole reason the set feels the way it does.
